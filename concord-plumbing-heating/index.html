Lake-effect cold. What to do now.

When the wind comes off the lake, pipes on outside walls go first. Pick what's happening at your house.

Before it hits

  1. Disconnect garden hoses and shut off and drain outside spigots.
  2. Open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls so warm air reaches the pipes.
  3. Let a faucet on an outside wall drip overnight.
  4. Keep the heat at 55°F or more, even if you're away.
  5. Know where your main water shut-off is before you need it.
Book a winter check

No water from a tap

  1. Leave the faucet open, so water can flow as it thaws.
  2. Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or space heater. Never an open flame.
  3. Start near the faucet and work back toward the frozen part.
  4. Can't reach it, or see a crack or bulge? Shut off the main water valve and call us.
Call 814-725-9812

Water is coming out

  1. Shut off the main water valve now.
  2. If water is near outlets or the panel, switch off that power at the breaker.
  3. Open the faucets to drain what's left in the pipes.
  4. Call us. We answer 24 hours a day.

Questions we hear

A pipe froze. What should I do?

Leave the faucet open and warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or space heater, never an open flame. If you can't reach it or it has split, shut off the main water valve and call 814-725-9812.
