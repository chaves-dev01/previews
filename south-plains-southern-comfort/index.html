Lubbock heating and AC questions

How much does an HVAC check cost in Lubbock?

Our integrity check is $105. We come out and check your system thoroughly before the cold nights arrive. Repairs are quoted after we find the cause.

My furnace is not working. What should I check first?

Check the thermostat batteries and setting, the filter, and the breaker or switch on the furnace. If the heater still won't come on, or you smell gas, call us.

My AC isn't cooling but the fan is running. What should I check?

Check that the thermostat is set to cool, replace a dirty filter, and look for a tripped breaker on the outdoor unit. If it still blows warm air, turn it off and call us so the compressor is not damaged.

How often should I change my filter?

Every one to three months. A dirty filter makes your system work harder, raises your energy bill and cuts the airflow.
