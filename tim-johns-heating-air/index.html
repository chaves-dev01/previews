Heating & AC questions

My heater won't turn on. What should I check before calling?

Make sure the thermostat is set to heat and its batteries are good, check that the filter is not clogged, and check the breaker or switch for the furnace. If it still won't run, call 806-535-2339.

How often should I change my air filter?

Check it every month and replace it when it looks dirty, usually every one to three months. A clean filter keeps the system efficient and energy bills low.

Do I need to clear leaves around my outdoor unit?

Yes. Leaves, grass and debris can collect around the outdoor unit and block the airflow. Keep a couple of feet clear around it.

Do you work on mobile homes?

Yes. We do residential, commercial and mobile home heating and air.

When should I schedule HVAC maintenance?

Twice a year: in the fall before heating season and in the spring before the Lubbock heat.
