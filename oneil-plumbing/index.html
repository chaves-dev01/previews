Questions

Five things Billings homeowners ask before they call. Anything else: 406-671-2537.

Can you fix my home inspection list?

Yes. A recent customer called for an evaluation of items from a home inspection and wrote that Reno was very thorough and explained what he was doing as he went.

Do I need a pressure reducing valve?

It lowers the pressure coming into the house. One customer wrote that the valve O'Neil put in back in 2024 saved their sprinkler system.

Can the water shut itself off?

Yes. O'Neil installs Moen Flo smart leak detection, which shuts the water off when it senses a leak, and you can control it from your phone.

Do you remodel old bathrooms?

Yes, including a historic district remodel with a freestanding tub, a custom shower and a double vanity.
