Plumbing questions

A pipe burst in my house. What do I do first?

Shut off the main water valve, turn off the water heater, open a faucet to drain the line, then call 806-441-1610.

How do I keep my pipes from freezing in a Lubbock cold snap?

Cover outdoor faucets, let indoor faucets on outside walls drip on the coldest nights, and open the cabinet doors under sinks so warm air reaches the pipes.

Can a water heater be repaired, or does it need replacing?

Many problems can be repaired. If the tank itself is leaking, it needs replacing. We tell you which before any work starts.

Do you do backflow testing in Lubbock?

Yes. We test and repair backflow devices for homes and businesses.
