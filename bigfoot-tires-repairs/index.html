Follow the green footprint

Bigfoot Tires & Repairs has kept trucks moving across the Texas Panhandle for four years. What started as one shop on East Amarillo Boulevard is now three, in Claude, Vega and Merkel, with Vega and Merkel right off the interstate, because that is where trucks break down. The crew wears a green footprint on the back of the shirt, so you know who is walking up to your rig. We repair tractors, trailers and diesel engines, sell new and used tires, wash out trailers and move the load when a truck can't go on. The phone is answered day and night. Call 806-379-7161.
