No heat? Check these first.

Some no-heat calls are a two-minute fix. Go through these, one at a time. If none of them works, we'll come out.

  1. 1

    Thermostat

    Is it set to Heat, and a few degrees above the room? Does the screen work, or are the batteries dead?

  2. 2

    Furnace filter

    A clogged filter can make a furnace overheat and shut itself off. Pull it out. If it's grey and you can't see light through it, swap it.

  3. 3

    Switch and breaker

    The furnace has a switch like a light switch, usually on or near it. Check it's on, and that its breaker hasn't tripped.

  4. 4

    Boiler pressure

    Have a boiler? Look at the gauge. On most homes it should read about 12 to 15 psi when cold. Much lower, or much higher, and it needs a technician.

Time to call us

You've checked the easy things. Call 814-899-2809 and tell us what you saw. If you smell gas, leave the house first and call from outside.

Erie heating questions

My furnace is not working. What should I check first?

Thermostat on Heat and set above room temperature, a clean filter, and the furnace switch and breaker on. The checklist above walks you through it. If it still won't run, or you smell gas, call 814-899-2809.

Do you repair boilers?

Yes. Boiler service, repair and replacement, including in-floor radiant heating systems.

Yes. Geothermal furnaces and ground loops.
